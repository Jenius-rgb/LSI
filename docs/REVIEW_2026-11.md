Purpose: recall SENTINEL's principles and find repo sections that conflict with facts published 2 and 10 Nov 2026. Fact references: `docs/official/README.md` and `board/BOARD_INFO.md`.

## Enduring principles

1. Golden model Python is the oracle; RTL must be bit-exact.
2. MAC lanes on fast clock are guarded by checksums; single-lane errors are corrected, remaining fall back to recalculation on safe clock.
3. In-FPGA governor selects clock per layer.
4. No claims of hardware behavior from simulation, synthetic models, or Vivado reports. Every number labeled.
5. Specifications change only via ADR.

No new facts invalidate these principles. The official chip is identical to the part most tested in M11.

## What did not change despite being checked

- Integer arithmetic, checkers, governor, register map, and SPI protocol do not depend on part or pins.
- M11 results for `xc7s25csga225-1` remain valid as [VIVADO-STA] for `engine` module; speed grade −2 and other part results become comparison data only.
- Target category remains Class 2 Level 3 [PROPOSAL]; Class 2 is now officially "ZERO7 only, free task, Level 3 only".

## New risks

1. **I/O voltages not yet written.** `board/zero7.xdc` assumes LVCMOS33. Bitstream for board awaits official `Zero7.xdc`.
2. **Synthesis report format not yet explained.** Official flow archives standard Vivado reports post-synthesis and post-implementation, so whatever format requested can be fulfilled.
3. **Complete design never implemented.** M11 numbers only for `engine`; 20 MHz clock, MMCM, and SPI could change utilization and timing.
4. **Novelty claims.** See `docs/research/RISET_WEB_2026-11.md` section (d).
